The Infamous Snipe

Stumbling in disrepair, Balthazar Greeves moved jerkily towards the water. Such a harmless thing, water. This pond in particular had always looked peaceful and calm. Up until now though, Bart had never been injured on one of his excursions. Sure, he had received a scratch or two while climbing random rocky bluffs, and who hadn’t experienced being in the woods with a dysfunctional ankle? But never before had he been mauled by a giant bird of prey. Oh..the pain!

The water called to him. Bart knelt down, intending to wash the wound on his side. But he couldn’t. The water demanded to remain still, even going so far as to show his reflection scrunching his eyebrows and shaking his head in silent condemnation. The water radiated calmness and the quiet determination that nothing could disturb its borders. It would sooner call upon the powers of the ancient world than a single ripple pass through its surface. Unfortunately for Bart, he was a practical man and feared the infection of his side and the rages of a dramatic emu rather than the quiet threat of determined H2o. Ah well, his funeral.

With a thunderous crash behind him, the Snipe pulled up short, stopping before it touched the water. But it was too late, Bart had leapt. Into the water he dove and ironically dove out of. To the average passerby his movement would look like he had mysteriously gracefully dived out of the pond rather than in. Landing on the bank in bewilderment, Balthazar turned to look back at his reflection desperately fending off the Snipe, a look of horror on his face as he did so. Whatever was happening, Bart was sure it shouldn’t be. Checking his phone for the time, he decided to text for assistance. He wrote “Warning, Snipe hunt gone wrong!!! Send back evac immediately!”. Clicking send, he moved his phone around trying to procure the best signal possible. Nothing.

With a careless toss, his phone went into the pond and his mind elsewhere. Where was he? Why hadn’t the phone worked? Why on earth had the movie UP made the snipe so tame in its appearance? It made no mention of its thrashing talons and razor beak! Anyway, his reflected self was feeling the full brunt of this ignorance as he ran circles around the indignant pond, disappearing beneath him and appearing again farther along the circle. Surely any normal beast would have calmed down by now. Not the snipe. Balthazar grimaced as a particularly nasty slice was made across his dollganger’s back. “Ouch, wouldn’t want to be him” wound its way slowly around his head before he realized the absurdity of such a statement. Maybe he should pick up a safer hobby, like poker or something.

As Baltazar slowly distanced his mind from the situation, a creature of unnerving nature noticed his mental slumber and slowly stalked up behind him. Quietly as an owl another Snipe crept up behind him readying his beak for the final strike. Tipped off only by the slight smell of maple butter the bird exuded, he whirled around, startling the beast and causing it to trip backwards and extend its wings for balance. A gust of wind hurled Bart out and into the water. On his way down, he hit the reflected form of himself, who had apparently also been tossed through the liquid barrier at the same time. His mind took a second to register the fact that he was back where he should be, but that was all the Snipe needed. Darting in quickly, the bird was interrupted by the smallest of details. Bart’s phone was ringing. As if it was a frightened cat, the Snipe hit reverse and stood stock still with its feathers up. Just in time for Bart’s back up to arrive.

A deuce and a half came roaring to his aid and only stopped after it met the Snipe’s tail end. With a puff of feathers and a sound akin to a western cowboy, the green vested driver halted his ride and opened the door.

“Need a ride?” came Edgar’s British voice from inside. “Also, was that the Snipe? ‘Cause I thought they were small.”

“Not now! Now drive!” Bart hopped up and pushed Edgar into the passenger seat. Popping the vehicle into reverse, Bart floored it and promptly doused the bird in dirt before finally moving backwards. “This thing is impossible to kill! I put five rounds in him already and nothing happened!!!”

“That checks out” came Edgar's reply as evaluated the dirt covered feathery mass before him, “ ‘cause he's getting up and I had to be doing at least forty when I hit him. Anyway, you’d better turn around before we hit that tree.”

Taking his advice, Bart spun the deuce around movie style. Not a small feat for such a large vehicle. Flooring it again, the two finally got up to speed right as the birdbrain, more like an Emu than a traditional Snipe, started running furiously towards the humans. Unfortunately for it, a military grade eight wheel drive vehicle is too fast for a two legged animal.

Bart looked over at his compatriot as he drove. “Homeword bound! Edgar that beast is amazing! Those Australian Emu wars were no joke! Imagine if we had ten of these things at our command..I wouldn’t want to be on my bad side, I’ll tell you that. But maybe next time we should go snark hunting.”
